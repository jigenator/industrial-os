# Mission

Give people working in Herdr a place to think beside their agents: sticky notes and free text on a board that belongs to the workspace, opened beside your panes or in its own tab, driven by the mouse, with a keyboard path for everything.

## Non-goals

No agent control, signal collection, sharing, sync, collaboration or export. No freehand drawing, images, rich text or undo history yet; add them only for a real need. The board never reads or changes another pane, Herdr's layout or the user's configuration.

It is one of the small Herdr workspace tools in the [repository mission](../../../docs/mission.md), and follows the shared visual language rather than a theme of its own.
